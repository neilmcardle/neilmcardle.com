# Scorecard: A History of Design Tools

Every tool considered for this history, scored 0–2 on five questions. Score 6 or more, or 2 on influence, puts a tool in the main story; 3–5 puts it under Also notable; 0–2 leaves it out. Tools under about two years old are listed To watch, because their influence cannot be judged yet. Scores are judgement calls, written down so they can be argued with.

- **Influence**: did later tools copy its ideas?
- **First**: did it introduce something new?
- **Reach**: how many designers used it at its peak?
- **Turning point**: did it trigger an industry event (acquisition, shutdown, pricing change)?
- **Explains today**: does knowing it help explain the tools people use now?
