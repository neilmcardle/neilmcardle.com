# makeEbook

A browser editor where authors write a book, review it with Book Mind and export it as an ebook for stores like Amazon, Apple Books and Kobo.

## Language

### The work

**Book**:
The thing an author creates and keeps in their library: a title, author, cover and chapters.
_Avoid_: Project, document, draft, work, ebook

**Manuscript**:
The text of a book, all its chapters read as one. What an author imports and what Book Mind reads.
_Avoid_: Content, body, text

**Ebook**:
The file an author exports from a book, mainly EPUB, and loosely PDF and Word. Never the thing being edited.
_Avoid_: Book (for the exported file), output

### A book's parts

**Chapter**:
Any entry in a book's list, with its own title and text. A dedication or an index is a chapter too.
_Avoid_: Section, page, entry

**Front matter**:
The kind of chapter that comes before the story or argument, such as a dedication, preface or foreword.

**Body**:
The kind of chapter that carries the story or argument itself.
_Avoid_: Content, main content

**Back matter**:
The kind of chapter that comes after the story or argument, such as endnotes, a bibliography or an author biography.

**Part**:
A chapter that introduces the chapters after it. A divider, not a container.

**Endnote**:
A numbered reference in the text to a note collected at the back of the book.
_Avoid_: Footnote

### Book Mind

**Chat**:
One conversation between an author and Book Mind about a book.
_Avoid_: Session, conversation, thread

**Question**:
One message an author sends to Book Mind in a chat.
_Avoid_: Prompt, query, request

**Book Mind note**:
A fact an author gives Book Mind to keep in mind for one book.
_Avoid_: Memory, instruction, context

### Checking a book

**Pre-flight**:
The checklist that confirms a book meets a store's requirements before it is exported. No AI is involved.
_Avoid_: Validation, lint, audit, check

**Pre-flight check**:
One item on the pre-flight checklist.

**Review**:
Book Mind reading part of a manuscript and commenting on it, such as a grammar or chronology review.
_Avoid_: Check, analysis, scan

### Book Mind without Pro

**Manuscript brief**:
Book Mind's reading of a whole manuscript, written once and used to answer questions about the book.
_Avoid_: Summary, memory, analysis

**Free question**:
The one Book Mind question an author without Pro may ask.
_Avoid_: Trial, free try

**Allowance**:
What Book Mind gives an author without Pro: one free question, and one manuscript brief a month.
_Avoid_: Grant, credit, quota, trial

### Keeping work safe

**Library**:
The books an author has saved.
_Avoid_: Shelf, collection, projects

**Save**:
Keeping a book on this device.
_Avoid_: Store, persist

**Cloud sync**:
The Pro feature that copies saved books to an author's account so they appear on every device.
_Avoid_: Upload, cloud save, backup

**Sync conflict**:
The same book changed on two devices before they synced.

**Version**:
A copy of a whole book as it was at one save, made automatically.
_Avoid_: Snapshot, revision, checkpoint

**History**:
Where a book's versions and its past exports are kept.

**Backup file**:
A file an author downloads with their books in it, for safekeeping or moving them.
_Avoid_: Export (an export makes an ebook)

### Paying

**Pro**:
What unlocks Book Mind beyond the allowance, and cloud sync.
_Avoid_: Premium, paid tier, tier, entitlement

**Free**:
Using makeEbook without Pro.
_Avoid_: Basic, starter

**Plan**:
How an author pays for Pro: a subscription or Lifetime.

**Subscription**:
The monthly plan. It can start with the Pro trial.

**Pro trial**:
A free first period of a subscription, with a card on file. The only meaning of "trial".
_Avoid_: Trial (for the free question)

**Lifetime**:
The plan that gives Pro for good after one payment.
_Avoid_: Licence, LTD, lifetime deal

**Grandfathered**:
An early author given Lifetime without paying. To the author it is simply Lifetime.
_Avoid_: Legacy, founder, early supporter
